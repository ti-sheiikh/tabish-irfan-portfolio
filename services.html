ENGINEERING SERVICES

Five specialist disciplines. One coordinated product workflow.

PCB reverse engineering, new electronics design, microcontroller firmware, mobile applications and mechanical product design are separate capabilities that can also be combined when a product needs end-to-end development.

01 / Reverse Engineering

PCB Reverse Engineering & Recreation

For obsolete, undocumented, damaged or unavailable electronics. We reconstruct the design into editable engineering files and identify practical paths for repair, redesign or controlled reproduction.

  • Schematic extraction
  • BOM & component identification
  • PCB recreation and Gerber outputs
  • Obsolescence and redesign support
Explore reverse engineering
02 / Electronics Design

PCB & Circuit Design

From requirements and architecture to schematic, layout, DFM and manufacturing packages for prototypes and production runs.

  • Analog, digital and mixed-signal circuits
  • Power, MCU, sensors and communications
  • Multilayer PCB layout and grounding strategy
  • BOM, Gerber, drill and CPL/PnP
Explore PCB design
03 / Firmware

Embedded Systems & Firmware

This service is the software that runs on the microcontroller: device drivers, sensors, control logic, protocols, connectivity, real-time behavior and hardware bring-up. Mobile UI/UX is handled separately under App Development.

  • ESP32, STM32, nRF52, RP2040, Arduino and related MCUs
  • Sensor and actuator drivers, calibration and state logic
  • BLE, Wi-Fi, LoRa/LoRaWAN, CAN, UART, SPI, I²C and USB
  • FreeRTOS, diagnostics, fault handling and hardware testing
Explore firmware development
04 / Applications

Mobile App Development & UI/UX

A separate application-development service for polished user interfaces, backend connectivity and real-device integration. Apps can be delivered independently or connected to Revorix-designed embedded hardware.

  • Flutter & Dart cross-platform applications
  • UI/UX flows for Android and iOS
  • Firebase, authentication, databases, cloud functions and APIs
  • BLE/Wi-Fi hardware control, telemetry and real-device testing
Explore app development
05 / Mechanical

3D CAD, Enclosures & Product Design

Mechanical design around real electronics: accessible connectors, mounting, buttons, ventilation, battery placement and prototype manufacturability.

  • Fusion 360 CAD and enclosure development
  • STL/STEP deliverables
  • PCB-to-enclosure integration
  • 3D-print and assembly considerations
Explore product design
