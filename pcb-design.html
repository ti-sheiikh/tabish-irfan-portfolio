Schematic engineering

WORKFLOW

How this work is approached.

  1. 01

    Define requirements

    Voltage, interfaces, dimensions, connectors, environment, cost and manufacturing targets.

  2. 02

    Schematic & review

    Architecture is captured into the schematic and checked before expensive layout effort.

  3. 03

    Layout & DFM

    Placement, routing, planes, mechanical constraints and production rules are developed.

  4. 04

    Release package

    Source, fabrication, assembly and documentation files are exported and cross-checked.
