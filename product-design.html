WORKFLOW

How this work is approached.

  1. 01

    Lock the electronics envelope

    Confirm PCB dimensions, connectors, battery, antenna and moving/mechanical elements.

  2. 02

    Develop the enclosure

    Build the assembly around access, ergonomics, fastening and manufacturing constraints.

  3. 03

    Fit-check digitally

    Review clearances, travel, interference, opening sizes and assembly sequence.

  4. 04

    Prototype & iterate

    Use the first physical print/fit check to capture tolerances before finalizing production intent.
