CAPABILITIES

Technical depth where the project needs it.

01

Connectivity reconstruction

Trace nets, planes, component relationships and interfaces from physical evidence and available documentation.

02

Component identification

Identify packages, values, markings and likely function; document uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.

03

PCB recreation

Rebuild board geometry, placement and routing with manufacturing constraints and mechanical dimensions in mind.

04

Redesign support

Replace obsolete parts, correct known weaknesses and adapt interfaces while preserving intended function.

WORKFLOW

How this work is approached.

  1. 01

    Collect evidence

    Board photos, measurements, scans, datasheets, working samples and known behavior are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Reconstruct

    Connectivity, functions and component relationships are rebuilt into an editable schematic and BOM.

  3. 03

    Recreate & review

    PCB geometry and routing are reconstructed, then checked with ERC/DRC and manufacturing rules.

  4. 04

    Validate & hand off

    Files are packaged with documentation, open questions and prototype/verification recommendations.

Project ownership & authorization

For reverse engineering or third-party hardware, clients are expected to own the hardware or have authorization to analyze, repair or redesign it. Revorix does not accept work intended to violate third-party intellectual-property rights.
