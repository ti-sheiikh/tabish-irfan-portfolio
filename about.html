FOUNDER-LED ENGINEERING

Built from years of hands-on freelance product work.

Founded by Electrical Engineer Tabish Irfan, Revorix Engineering brings together PCB design, reverse engineering, embedded systems, IoT and mechanical product development.

The company model is intentionally practical: understand the real hardware, define interfaces clearly, design with manufacturing in mind and deliver editable files that the client can continue using after the project ends.

Work can begin with a single legacy PCB or evolve into a complete product spanning electronics, firmware, enclosure and companion software.

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES

What the Revorix name should stand for.

01

Evidence over assumption

Unknowns are documented, measured and reduced before they become expensive design decisions.

02

Design for the next stage

Prototype work should not make manufacturing, servicing or future revisions unnecessarily difficult.

03

Editable handoff

Clients should receive usable engineering sources and documentation, not only rendered outputs.

04

Respect confidentiality

Project material is treated as client engineering data and public portfolio use is intentionally selective.
